# French course: A2 to C2 (#4)

Date: 2026-10-17
Issue: https://github.com/ozerozdas/sprigo/issues/4

## Goal

Learners who finish French A1 can go on to C2, as in Spanish (#3, PR #65). Content only: five new levels in `courses/fr.yml`; the AI writes exercises and guidebooks from them.

## Decisions

- **Same shape as A1 and es.yml:** every level has 10 units × 4 steps and a checkpoint (A1's activities plus `error_correct`).
- **Guidebooks need no YAML:** `loadGuide` builds each unit's guide from its vocabulary and grammar.
- **Language:** titles and descriptions in English, `vocabulary` and `grammar.pattern` in French.
- **Grammar is French, not translated Spanish or English.** A1 already covers the passé composé, futur proche and pouvoir + infinitive, so A2 builds on them.
- **Step ids** are unique across the file.
