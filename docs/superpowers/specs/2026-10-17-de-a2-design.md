# German course: A2 to C2 (#5)

Date: 2026-10-17
Issue: https://github.com/ozerozdas/sprigo/issues/5

## Goal

German runs A1–C2, like Spanish (#3, PR #65). Content only: five new level blocks in `courses/de.yml`; the AI writes exercises and guidebooks from them.

## Decisions

- **Same shape as A1:** each level has 10 units × 4 steps and a checkpoint (A1's activities plus `error_correct` ×2).
- **Guidebooks need no YAML:** `loadGuide` builds them from each unit's vocabulary and grammar.
- **Language:** titles and descriptions in English, `vocabulary` and `grammar.pattern` in German.
- **Grammar is German, not translated English or Spanish:** A1 already covers Perfekt, war/hatte, können/möchten, separable verbs and nominative articles, so A2 starts with the cases (Akkusativ, Dativ, Wechselpräpositionen), verb-final clauses and adjective endings. Topics follow the Goethe-Zertifikat levels.
- **Step and unit ids** of A2–C2 start with the level (`a2-…`, `c1-…`), so they cannot clash with A1 or each other.
- Every unit's 4th step ends with a `dialogue`.

## Units

- **A2 Elementary:** Jobs & Work (müssen/dürfen/sollen, *seit*) · Comparisons (Komparativ, *so … wie*, Superlativ) · At the Shops (Akkusativ, pronouns, adjective endings) · Health & the Body (*Mir tut … weh*, Imperativ) · Travel & Places (Wechselpräpositionen, *hätte gern / könnten Sie*) · Gifts & Celebrations (Dativ, Dativ verbs) · Reasons & Opinions (*weil/dass*, verb last) · Feelings & Daily Life (reflexive verbs) · When I Was a Child (Präteritum of modals, *als*) · Plans & Invitations (*werden*, *wenn*, time prepositions)
- **B1 Intermediate:** Telling Stories (Präteritum, Plusquamperfekt) · Wishes & Dreams (Konjunktiv II) · Arguments (*obwohl, trotzdem, deshalb*) · Advice & Polite Requests (indirect questions) · Purpose (*um … zu, damit*, *zu*-infinitive) · Relative Clauses · Passive · Work & Careers (Genitiv, *wegen/trotz*) · Lifestyle (verbs with prepositions, *da-/wo-*) · Media & Technology (n-Deklination)
- **B2 Upper Intermediate:** Regrets & Hypotheticals (Konj. II past) · Deduction & Speculation (subjective modals, Futur II) · Passive & Alternatives · Reported Speech (Konj. I) · Linking Ideas (two-part connectors) · Participles as Adjectives · Nominal Style & Noun–Verb Phrases · Debate & Persuasion · Society & Environment · Science, Culture & the Arts
- **C1 Advanced:** Word Order & Emphasis · Modal Particles · Extended Participle Phrases · Advanced Connectors · Hedging & Discourse · Professional Life · Media & Information · Science, Technology & Ethics · Society & Politics · Idioms & Colloquial German
- **C2 Proficiency:** Register · Regional Varieties (D/A/CH) · Modality & Stance · Figurative Language · Rhetoric · Humour, Irony & Wordplay · Academic & Formal Writing · Literature & Style · Ideas & Philosophy · Mastery & Mediation

## Tests

`src/course.test.ts`: `de` in `LEVELS` becomes A1–C2 (10 × 4 and a checkpoint per level, range `A1–C2`).
