# Turkish course: A2 to C2 (#6)

Date: 2026-10-17
Issue: https://github.com/ozerozdas/sprigo/issues/6

## Goal

Learners who finish Turkish A1 can continue to C2, as in Spanish (#3, PR #65). Content only: new `A2:`…`C2:` blocks in `courses/tr.yml`; the AI writes exercises and guidebooks from them.

## Decisions

- **Same shape as es A2–C2:** each level has 10 units × 4 steps and a checkpoint (A1's activities plus `error_correct` ×2).
- **Guidebooks need no YAML:** `loadGuide` builds them from vocabulary and grammar.
- **Language:** titles and descriptions in English, `vocabulary` and `grammar.pattern` in Turkish.
- **Turkish grammar spine, not translated English or Spanish:** suffix chains and vowel harmony throughout; case suffixes (accusative, ablative), aorist, -(y)AcAk, -mIş evidentiality, -DIK/-(y)An participles, converbs (-Ip, -ArAk, -IncA, -ken), passive/causative, conditionals (-sA, -sAydI), register (sen/siz) and Ottoman/öz Türkçe vocabulary at C2.
- **Step ids** are unique across the file.
- **tr.yml header:** A1 starts at line 12; the header comment says "A1 only" and is updated.

## Units

### A2 Elementary
1. Jobs & Work: professions, `-DIr`, aorist for routines (*Genelde erken kalkarım*), `-DEn beri`
2. Comparisons: `-DEn daha`, `en`, `kadar`, `gibi`
3. At the Shops: accusative `-(y)I`, ablative `-DEn`, quantities, `bunu/şunu`
4. Health & the Body: *Başım ağrıyor*, `-mAlI`, polite imperative `-(y)In`, *Geçmiş olsun*
5. Travel Plans: `-mAk istiyorum`, `-mAyı düşünüyorum`, booking, `-(y)A kadar`
6. The Future: `-(y)AcAk` (positive, negative, question), time words
7. When I Was a Child: `-ken`, past habit `-(I)rdI`, describing the past
8. Telling Stories: `-(I)yordu`, `-DIktAn sonra`, `-mAdAn önce`, connectors
9. Experiences & Hearsay: *Hiç … gittin mi?*, `daha önce`, first contact with `-mIş`
10. Invitations & Rules: aorist offers (*Çay içer misin?*), `-(y)AlIm mı?`, `-(y)Ebilir miyim?`, `yasak`, `-mAk zorunda`

### B1 Intermediate
Past Tenses Together (-DI/-mIş/-(I)yordu/-mIştI) · Real Conditionals (-sA, -(I)rsA) · Wishes & Regrets (keşke, -sAydI) · Opinions (bence, -DIğInI düşünüyorum) · Reported Speech (-DIğInI söyledi, diye) · Relative Clauses (-(y)An, -DIK) · Advice & Requests (-sAn iyi olur, -mAsInI istiyorum) · Work & Careers (-mAk için, -DIğI için) · Habits & Lifestyle (-mAyA başlamak, -mAyı bırakmak, alışmak) · Media & Technology (-ArAk, -(y)IncA)

### B2 Upper Intermediate
Passive Voice (-Il/-In) · Causative (-DIr/-t) · Hypotheticals (-sAydI … -(I)rdI) · Deduction & Speculation (-mIş olmalı, -DIr, herhalde) · Time Clauses (-DIğIndA, -DIkçA, -(I)r -mAz, -(y)AlI) · Linking Ideas (-mAsInA rağmen, -DIğI halde, oysa) · Purpose & Reason (-sIn diye, -DIğIndAn) · Debate & Persuasion · Society & the Environment · Science, Culture & the Arts

### C1 Advanced
Evidentiality in Depth (-mIşmIş, -mIş gibi) · Participles & Nominalisation (-(y)AcAk olan, -Iş, -mA) · Emphasis & Word Order (focus, ki, ise, bile) · Converbs (-Ip, -mAdAn, -cAsInA) · Hedging & Discourse (sanki, galiba, -DIğI söylenebilir) · Professional Life (-mAktAdIr, formal register) · Media & Information (-DIğI bildirildi) · Science, Technology & Ethics · Idioms & Colloquial Turkish · Argument & Academic Writing

### C2 Proficiency
Register & Politeness (siz, efendim, rica etsem, teşrif) · Modality & Stance (-(y)Iver, -(y)Ayaz, -mAk üzere, -(y)AsI) · Figurative Language (deyimler) · Proverbs & Sayings (atasözleri) · Rhetoric & Persuasion · Humour, Irony & Wordplay (Nasreddin Hoca) · Academic & Formal Writing · Literature & Style (Yunus Emre, Nazım Hikmet, archaic forms) · Language & Identity (dil devrimi, öz Türkçe vs Arabic/Persian loans, dialects) · Mastery & Mediation
